    Studio notes

    Get the most from every metre.

    1. 01

      Use transparent PNGs

      DTF prints white ink under every pixel that isn't fully transparent. Remove backgrounds before uploading so you don't get a printed box around your design.

    2. 02

      Set the final print width

      The width you enter is the size it presses on the garment. Measure the area on the shirt — the size guide gives typical widths.

    3. 03

      Rotation saves money

      Letting designs turn 90° often fits more prints per row and shortens the sheet, which lowers the price.

      Need a hand?
