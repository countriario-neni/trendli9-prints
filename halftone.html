The why

Why halftone for DTF?

  1. Breathable, soft hand-feel

    Dots instead of a solid sheet of ink and adhesive mean less film on the garment — it flexes, breathes and feels lighter to wear.

  2. No hard box edges

    Glows, shadows and faded edges can't print as "half-transparent" in DTF. Dots recreate the fade, so you don't get a visible rectangle around the design.

  3. Cleaner on dark shirts

    Removing the black background and letting the shirt show through the dots keeps dark designs crisp, without a muddy grey patch.
