FAQ

Questions, answered

What is DTF printing and which fabrics does it work on?
DTF (Direct-to-Film) prints your design onto a PET film with white ink and adhesive powder, which is then heat-pressed onto the garment. It works on cotton, polyester, blends, rayon, silk, denim, nylon, fleece and even leather — on both light and dark colours.
How much does DTF transfer printing cost?
Gang sheets start at ₹250 per metre (58 cm wide). Ready-to-press single transfers start at ₹15 for logo size. Use the free Auto Gang Sheet tool for an instant price; the final quote is confirmed on WhatsApp.
Is there a minimum order quantity?
No minimum on custom apparel — single pieces are welcome. Gang sheets start from 1 metre, and ready-to-press transfers from 10 pieces. Bulk discounts start at 10 pieces.
How do I place an order and track it?
Add products or your custom design to the cart and check out. WhatsApp opens with your full order and a unique order ID — tap Send. Our team confirms the price and timeline in that chat, and your order summary is saved on the My Orders page.
Do you print on sarees and blouses?
Yes. We print motifs, borders and pallu art on sarees and neckline/back designs on blouses across silk, georgette, chiffon and cotton.
Do you sell DTF inks, film and spare parts?
Yes — C/M/Y/K/W inks, PET film sheets and rolls, hot-melt powder, printheads (XP600, i3200, L1800), dampers, capping & wiper kits and cleaning solution, with technical guidance.
Do you deliver outside Visakhapatnam?
Yes. You can pick up from our studio at Dabagardens, Visakhapatnam, or choose courier delivery anywhere in India.
